What We Collect

  • User IDs - Unique Discord identifiers
  • Guild IDs - Server identifiers where the bot is active
  • Security Logs - Records of violations and exploit activity

How We Use Data

Data is used strictly to keep communities safe and identify users in violation of Discord or Roblox Terms of Service. This includes:

  • Checking new members against records of verified ToS violators
  • Sending alerts to moderators about potential threats
  • Keeping records of security violations

Data Retention & Deletion

We will delete your personal data upon request. However, we retain records of users documented to have violated Discord or Roblox Terms of Service, as deletion would prevent detection of repeat offenders and undermine platform safety. If you believe you were flagged in error, you can file an appeal through our support server and we will review your case. Successful appeals result in immediate data deletion.

We recognize that security researchers, investigators, or innocent users may occasionally be flagged. If you believe your data was logged in error, you can easily file an appeal through our support server. Each case is reviewed manually.

Data Storage

  • Stored on secure servers with restricted access
  • Access is strictly limited to bot administrators
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Contact

For questions, data deletion requests, or to file an appeal, join our support server.

What Cozine Does

Cozine is a tool for moderators. It identifies users in violation of Discord or Roblox Terms of Service and sends alerts. We are not responsible for individual server ban decisions. Moderators make the final call.

Acceptable Use

  • Use the bot only for legitimate server moderation
  • Do not use the bot to harass or target users
  • Do not attempt to manipulate or abuse the alert system

Prohibited Actions

  • Using the bot outside of moderation purposes
  • Attempting to reverse engineer or extract data
  • Sharing false reports to get users flagged

Service Disclaimer

Cozine is provided "as-is" with no guarantees of uptime or accuracy. We are not liable for:

  • Server disruptions or downtime
  • Missed threats or false alerts
  • Actions taken by moderators based on bot alerts

Eligibility

  • Must be 13+ years old (Discord's minimum age)
  • Must comply with Discord's Terms of Service

For questions or appeals, join our support server.
